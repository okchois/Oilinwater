Rev J / one prototype set. M-105 and M-106: MJF PA12-HP Nylon, one each. T-001 tool: one.
Printed dimensions are nominal. JLC published as-printed tolerance +/-0.3 mm does NOT meet all drawing fits.
M-105/106: inspect and finish OD and PCB slots to drawing final dimensions before assembly.
M-105: two nominal dia1.6 pilot holes for M2x0.4. Check actual pilot size before tapping; oversized holes require reprint.
No printed thread helix. Do not assume thread strength or fit is validated. Do not overtighten.
Remove loose powder and burrs. If supplier does not offer finishing, receiver must finish and inspect.
Reject/reprint undersize external fits or oversized holes/slots that cannot be corrected. No unconditional drop-in fit claim.
Temperature, creep, epoxy compatibility and finished assembly require prototype tests. HDT is not continuous service temperature.
